Pricing questions

The details.

Why is there a monthly cost for the device?

The wearable has its own SIM so it can work without your phone. The monthly amount pays for that mobile connection.

Will the app stay free?

That is the plan. The app is how most people will first try SafeTag, and it needs no hardware from us.

When can I buy the device?

We don't have a date. The hardware is still being built and tested. Send us a message and we'll tell you when pre-orders open.

Are these prices final?

No. They are working figures while the device is in development.
