Terms and conditions

The rules for using the SafeTag test build and this website. Please read the part about emergencies.

Last updated 12 October 2026.

1. What SafeTag is

SafeTag is a final-year engineering project: an Android app, a server, and a wearable device that is still in development. What you can download from this site is a test build, shared so that friends, classmates and other volunteers can try it and tell us what they find.

2. SafeTag is not an emergency service

SafeTag sends alerts to people you have chosen. It does not contact the police, an ambulance or any other emergency service. Alerts can be delayed or fail to arrive, for example when there is no mobile signal, no internet connection, a flat battery, or a fault in the software.

Do not rely on SafeTag as your only way of getting help. In an emergency, call your local emergency services.

3. Using the test build

  • The test build is provided as it is, without any promise that it will work, be available, or be free of faults.
  • We may change it, take it offline, or delete all test data at any time, without notice.
  • Location is only as accurate as your phone's or the wearable's GPS, which can be wrong by tens of metres or more, especially indoors.

4. Your account

Give accurate details when you sign up, keep your password to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has used your account. You are responsible for what happens under your account.

5. Trusted contacts and consent

Only add people as trusted contacts with their agreement. Only watch another person's location with their knowledge and permission. Using SafeTag to track or monitor someone without their consent is not allowed, and we will remove accounts that do it.

6. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • send false alerts on purpose, or use SafeTag to harass or alarm anyone;
  • try to access other people's accounts or data;
  • interfere with the service, or attempt to overload it;
  • use SafeTag for anything unlawful.

7. The device and prices

The wearable is not on sale. Descriptions of it on this site describe what we are building and may change. Prices shown are planning figures, not offers, and no order can be placed.

8. Your information

How we handle your information is set out in the privacy policy, which forms part of these terms.

9. Our responsibility

SafeTag is made by students as an academic project and offered free of charge. To the extent the law allows, the SafeTag team is not liable for loss or harm arising from use of the test build, including an alert that is delayed, misdirected or not delivered.

10. Ending use

You can stop using SafeTag at any time by uninstalling the app, and you can ask us to delete your account. We may suspend or remove an account that breaks these terms.

11. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the project develops. The date at the top of the page shows when they last changed.

12. Governing law
