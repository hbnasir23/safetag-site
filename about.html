About us

We're building the button we wish everyone had.

SafeTag is a final-year engineering project. Four students and two supervisors, working on one problem: asking for help should take one press, not a phone call.

Why we started

Most safety apps assume you can take out your phone, unlock it, find the app and explain what's wrong. In the moments that matter, you often can't do any of those things.

We wanted something that works with a single press, that can stay silent when it needs to, and that keeps working when the phone is dead, locked or gone. That became SafeTag: a small wearable with its own connection, and an app that does the same job in the meantime.

Where we are

The app works today. It sends loud and silent SOS alerts, shares live location, follows planned journeys and watches safe zones. The server that receives alerts from the wearable is running and tested.

The wearable itself is still being built. Until it's ready, your phone plays its part.
