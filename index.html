How it works

From one press to people on their way.

The wearable and the app do the same job. Use whichever you can reach.

You press

On the SafeTag device, or the SOS button in the app. A double press sounds an alarm; a long hold stays completely silent.

SafeTag sends

Your position and the alert travel over the mobile network to our server in about two seconds.

Contacts are alerted

Their phones ring like a call, even on silent, and open a live map of where you are.

It keeps going

If the first person doesn't respond, the next one in your list is alerted, and so on down the chain.

Double press

Audible SOS

A loud alarm to draw attention and scare someone off, plus the alert to your contacts.

Hold 3 seconds

Silent SOS

No sound, no light. Nobody near you knows, but your contacts see and hear everything.

Features

Built for the moments when calling isn't possible.

Nearly all of this works in the app today, using your phone. The wearable does the same without one.

SOS, loud or silent

Two ways to call for help, chosen by how you press. Cancel it yourself the moment you're safe.

Live location

Contacts follow you on a map as you move, with the path you've taken, for as long as the alert is active.

Journey mode

Plan a route like on Google Maps, or record one. Leave it for 45 seconds and your contacts are alerted. Come back, and the alert closes itself.

Safe zones

Mark home, university, work. Contacts get a quiet notice when you arrive or leave, without an alarm.

Trusted contacts

Invite people with a link. You set the order they're alerted in, and exactly what each one may see.

Find them now

With the wearable, a contact you've allowed can ask it for its position on the spot, even while it's asleep to save battery.

The device In development

Works when your phone can't.

Dead battery, locked screen, phone snatched or out of reach: the SafeTag wearable has its own connection and its own GPS, on a key ring or a bag zip.

  • Its own SIMSends alerts over the mobile network and can place a call to your contacts, with no phone involved.
  • Built-in GPSReports where it is every few seconds during an SOS or a journey, and every few minutes otherwise.
  • Microphone, no speakerYour contacts can hear what's happening around you. Whoever is near you hears nothing back.
  • BuzzerA loud alarm for the audible SOS, and a short buzz if you drift off your planned route.
  • One buttonBig enough to find by feel in a pocket. Double press or hold: that's the whole interface.

Good to know

Questions people ask.

This is a test build for friends and classmates.

Do I need the device to use SafeTag?

No. The app turns your phone into the SafeTag, and every feature on this page works that way today. The wearable is for when you can't use your phone.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. The iOS app is in development. For now SafeTag is Android only.

Who can see my location?

Only the trusted contacts you add, and only what you allow each of them. During an SOS, every contact can see where you are, because that's the point of it.

Should I rely on this in a real emergency?

Not as your only way to get help. SafeTag is a student project in testing. Use it alongside calling emergency services, not in place of them.

Will my account be kept?

We reset the test server from time to time, so you may need to sign up again. Updating the app by installing a newer file over the old one keeps your account.

I found a bug. What now?

Tell whoever sent you this link what you did and what happened. Screenshots help a lot.
