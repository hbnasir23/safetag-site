SafeTag Test build

One press away from safe.

SafeTag sends an SOS and your live location to the people you trust, the moment you need them. No unlocking, no typing, no explaining.

Download for Android

Android 6 or newer · about 24 MB

What it does

Built for the moments when calling for help isn't possible.

SOS, loud or silent

Double-tap for an SOS with an alarm. Press and hold for a silent one that nobody around you can hear.

Live location

Your trusted contacts see where you are on a map, updating as you move, for as long as the alert is active.

Journey mode

Pick a route before you travel. If you leave it, or don't arrive on time, your contacts are told automatically.

Safe zones

Mark places like home and university. Contacts get a quiet notice when you arrive or leave.

Trusted contacts

You choose who is alerted, in what order, and what each person is allowed to see.

A wearable, soon

We're building a small SafeTag device with its own SIM and GPS. Until it's ready, your phone does its job.

How to install

The app isn't on the Play Store yet, so Android asks for one extra permission.

  1. Download the fileTap Download for Android on your phone. Your browser may warn that the file could be harmful; choose "Download anyway".
  2. Open itTap the download notification, or find SafeTag.apk in your Downloads folder.
  3. Allow the installAndroid will say your browser isn't allowed to install apps. Tap Settings, switch on "Allow from this source", then go back and tap Install.
  4. Sign up and add a contactCreate an account with your email, allow location and notifications, then add a trusted contact. They need the app too.

Good to know

This is a test build for friends and classmates.

  • Android only. There's no iPhone version yet.
  • It's a student project in testing. Please don't rely on it as your only way to get help in a real emergency.
  • Test data may be wiped. We reset the test server from time to time, so you might need to sign up again.
  • To update, download the newest file from this page and install it over the old one. Your account stays.
