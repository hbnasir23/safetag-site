  • Live location every 5 s during an SOS

AreaIn the proposalWhat we builtWhy
CellularSIM800L 2G GSM moduleSIMCom A7670G 4G LTE Cat 1: data and voice at the same time2G could not send data during a call, and 2G networks are being phased out
ControllerESP32ESP32-S3-Zero, smaller and lower powerFits a keychain-sized device and saves battery

Device security

Each device logs in with its own serial and secret, and can only use its own MQTT topics.

Reliable alerts

The device resends the same event until the server acknowledges it; duplicates are ignored. One alert per event.

Escalation

Push to every contact at once; SMS escalates one contact at a time until someone responds.
